01Who we are

Freelance Web Developers is an independent digital solutions studio based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. This notice explains what information this website collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

02Information you choose to share

The enquiry form asks for your name, the service you need and a short project description. Business name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, estimated budget and preferred contact method are up to you — we only need one way to reply.

The “Send via WhatsApp” option does not send anything by itself. It opens WhatsApp with your message filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press Send in WhatsApp.

03How we use it

To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project; to prepare a quote; and, if you go ahead, to deliver the agreed work. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

04How enquiries reach us

When you press “Send enquiry”, the website passes your details over an encrypted connection to our email provider, which delivers them to our inbox. The website itself does not keep a database of enquiries.

05Third-party services

Hosting (Vercel) serves the website and may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type. Google Fonts supplies the website’s typefaces. Our email provider (Google Gmail) handles enquiry emails. WhatsApp (Meta) handles messages you send there. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

06Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own cookies and does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers. If we add analytics, it will load only after you agree, and this notice will be updated first.

07How long we keep information

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond. If you become a client, project records may be kept longer to support the work and to meet legal or accounting requirements.

08Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you have shared with us. Contact us using any of the details below and we will respond.

10Changes to this notice

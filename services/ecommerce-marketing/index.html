The service

Your Store, Marketplaces and Ads Are Different Jobs.

Building an online store is website development. Selling more through it, through marketplaces and through ads are separate jobs — each with its own costs. We keep them apart so you can see what is working.

  1. 01

    Your own store

    Optimising Shopify or WooCommerce stores: product pages, checkout, cart recovery and retention.

    CostOur fee (store builds: Website Development)

  2. 02

    Marketplaces

    Amazon and Flipkart listings, keywords, catalogue and marketplace ads.

    CostOur fee + marketplace fees

  3. 03

    Paid advertising

    Google Shopping feeds and Shopping campaigns.

    CostAd budget paid to the platform + management fee

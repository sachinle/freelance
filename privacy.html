Privacy

Privacy Notice.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This notice explains what information this website collects, how it is used and the choices you have. It applies to the Freelance Web Developers website, based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

Information you choose to share

The enquiry form asks for your name, the service you need and a short description of your project. You can also add a phone or WhatsApp number, an email address and an estimated budget.

  • WhatsApp button: the form does not store or send your details itself. It opens WhatsApp with your message written out, and the message is only sent if you press Send in WhatsApp.
  • Email or form service: if you use the “Send a Project Enquiry” option, your details are sent by email or through a third-party form service so we can receive your enquiry.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, to deliver the agreed work.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Third-party services

Some parts of this website are provided by other companies, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads:

  • Google Fonts, which supplies the website's typefaces.
  • Unsplash, which hosts some of the photographs shown on the website.
  • WhatsApp and your email provider, when you choose to contact us through them.

These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, this notice will be updated first.

How long we keep information

Enquiry messages are kept for as long as needed to respond and, for clients, to support the project and meet any legal or accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information you have shared with us by contacting us through the details on the contact section of the website.

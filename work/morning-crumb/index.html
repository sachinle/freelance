The brief

The Business Challenge.

A neighbourhood bakery sells out most mornings. Regulars phone ahead to reserve bakes, the menu changes daily, and new customers mostly find the shop on Google Maps from their phones.

The site needed to answer three questions in seconds — what is baked today, can I reserve it, and how do I get there — without the cost of a full online shop.

Scope of work

  • Information architecture
  • Mobile-first UI design
  • Daily menu module
  • Pickup pre-order form → WhatsApp
  • One-tap call & directions
  • Local SEO setup
  • Google Business Profile alignment
  • Analytics events plan

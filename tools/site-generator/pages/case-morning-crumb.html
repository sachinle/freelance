The brief

The Business Challenge.

A neighbourhood bakery sells out most mornings. Regulars phone ahead to reserve bakes, the menu changes daily, and new customers mostly find the shop on Google Maps from their phones.

The site needed to answer three questions in seconds — what is baked today, can I reserve it, and how do I get there — without the cost of a full online shop.

Scope of work

  • Information architecture
  • Mobile-first UI design
  • Daily menu module
  • Pickup pre-order form → WhatsApp
  • One-tap call & directions
  • Local SEO setup
  • Google Business Profile alignment
  • Analytics events plan

Approach

Approach & Design Decisions.

Three decisions shaped the concept — each one tied to how bakery customers actually behave on their phones.

  1. {{phone:crumb}}
    01

    Today’s menu comes first

    The first screen answers “what is baked today?” — updated from a simple list the owner can edit, no CMS training needed.

  2. {{phone:crumb}}
    02

    Pre-order in three taps

    Choose items, choose a pickup time, send. The order arrives as a structured WhatsApp message the bakery already reads.

  3. {{phone:crumb}}
    03

    Call and directions within reach

    A persistent call button and a Maps link stay one tap away, because most new customers arrive from Google Maps.
